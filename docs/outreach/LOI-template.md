# Letter of Intent — FX Regime Radar design partnership (one page, non-binding)

**DRAFT — not legal advice. Have a professional confirm Swiss FinSA specifics before use.**

**Between** {Company name, legal form, address} ("the Partner")
**and** {Operator name / legal entity} ("the Operator"), operator of FX Regime Radar.

## 1. Purpose
The Partner intends to use FX Regime Radar's Pro tier (weekly report, alerts on chosen pairs,
Treasury mode, monthly PDF) as an *educational decision-support tool* for its currency-risk
process. The tool describes market regimes, regime-change risk and anomaly levels; it never
predicts price direction and never gives investment advice.

## 2. Design-partner period
* Duration: six (6) months from {start date}, free of charge.
* The Partner commits to one feedback call of about 30 minutes per month and agrees that
  anonymised feedback may shape the product. No confidential Partner data is shared with the
  Operator; the tool needs none.

## 3. Intent after the period
If the Partner finds the tool useful at the end of the period, the Partner intends to subscribe
to the Pro tier at **CHF 79 per month**, billed monthly, cancellable at any time with one
month's notice. No annual commitment is requested or accepted. This section is a statement of
intent, not a purchase obligation.

## 4. Not advice, no warranty
The Partner acknowledges the standing notice on every surface: **"Educational tool. Not
investment advice."** The Operator makes no representation about future market outcomes; the
public forward-test ledger is the only performance statement and is provided as-is.

## 5. Data and confidentiality
The Operator stores the Partner's company name, a contact role and the subscription status —
nothing else (see docs/PRIVACY.md). Either party may end the design partnership at any time
with written notice; nothing in this letter is legally binding except this section and §4.
