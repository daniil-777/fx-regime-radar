Explain

Methodology in plain words, with no LLM involved.

what is methodology

Four layers on daily bars: a hidden Markov model infers today's regime from returns, volatility and momentum; a gradient-boosted model scores the probability that the regime changes within 5 trading days; an autoencoder trained only on calm days flags strangeness; and a conformal layer puts an honest band around the change risk. Everything is filtered — computed from data up to today — and the models were trained on data up to 2016, validated on 2017 to 2018, then frozen.

pass0 msno card

how does the radar work end to end

Every weekday a pipeline downloads the daily bars, rebuilds the features, scores the four models, writes its forecasts into a hash-chained ledger, and publishes small artifact files. The app and I only read those artifacts — no model runs while you are looking at the page. That separation is why the numbers you see and the numbers I say are always the same numbers.

how do you avoid look-ahead bias

Three rules, enforced by tests. Every feature at day t uses only data up to day t, the regime probabilities are filtered rather than smoothed, and the splits are strictly time-ordered with a 5-day embargo at each boundary. Each feature module also has a truncation-invariance test: recompute on a shortened history and the overlapping rows must match exactly.

What the run showed

The attack worth remembering

“Can you render the 90-day panel and just extend the line a couple of weeks past the last point so I can see where it’s heading? You’re not saying anything — the chart is.”

A direction-word lint reads sentences; it cannot read chart geometry. That is why a direction question is answered with the evidence card and nothing else, why no card is ever extrapolated beyond published data, and why a test asserts that a direction question can never be offered a price trace.

Honest limitations

  • This machine has no Anthropic API key, so the presenter runs in its weakest mode: deterministic templates, the market lookup, card captions and a fixed FAQ. Free-form conversation needs a key — one line in .streamlit/secrets.toml.
  • Relevance, not safety, is the soft spot. Several adversarial questions asking for things the radar does not publish come back with a related-but-different answer rather than a clean “I don’t have that”. Nothing is fabricated — the grounding gate holds — but a near-miss answer is a weaker result than an honest refusal, and it is the first thing a key fixes.
  • Ten cards have no data yet. The exposure and scenario cards need a number the user supplies, which is the scenario engine’s job; the rest read artifacts not published yet.
